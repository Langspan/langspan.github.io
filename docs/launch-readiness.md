Reviewed 25 November 2026 against core main fba8ff0673fe2efc4c72eb1041d00a809eb7fb29, release v0.1.0, website main 24d23580b1cead0a208633859af3e42e20d55f9f and the organization profile. Preserve the approved Swarm Weave artwork, paper/ink/coral identity, static architecture, working Stageshow and Living Canvas illustrations. No new tracking or service.

## Product truth and audience
Developers with several existing sessions on connected changes are the primary audience. Engineering leads evaluating collaboration and solo builders are secondary. Positioning hypothesis, not customer research: agent-level awareness before the next relevant decision is the differentiating value; worktree isolation, Git, manual coordination and orchestrators are alternatives/complements. Do not assert competitive exclusivity or conversion lift.

The website leads with the existing promise and concrete mechanism, then an illustrated local example, worktree/remote scope, actual retained evidence, mission and a release-first adoption path. No claim of cross-machine locks or measured savings. First action: download an archive, verify it, connect one checkout, inspect status. The four archives and checksum are present in the published release metadata; the Homebrew tap was not found.

## Market scan (public descriptions, 25 November 2026)

- [Cork](https://corkcli.com/blog/coordinate-ai-coding-agents-across-team) describes native agent coordination, cross-machine awareness and write gates on a paid team offering.
- [Coordinaut](https://github.com/LevDomasnih/coordinaut) describes task ownership, scoped claims, handoffs and hosted sync.
- [Kith](https://github.com/connorhriley/kith) describes local coordination, scoped claims, worktrees and verified integration.
- [AgentLane](https://github.com/thetrueshags/AgentLane) describes Git-native coordination with task lanes and exclusive path claims.

These are provider descriptions, not independent tests. Shared coordination is not a new category. LangSpan should compete on how early and usefully relevant context reaches an existing session, transparent enforcement boundaries, open-source deployment and adoption friction. The product has not established superiority or comparative time savings. Keep competitor claims out of the public homepage unless independently verified.
