# LangSpan artwork

The owner supplied the updated `langspan-brand-kit(1).zip` during the November
24, 2026 review. The ten SVGs and `COLORS.txt` here are byte-for-byte copies from
that kit. The archive SHA-256 is
`0c733700f7834df0b4498fd4bf588360d10e0a33b10596cd8f3ecf106b969d0b`.

The kit contains six interleaved ribbons as filled outlines and an outlined
wordmark. The lettering has no installed-font dependency. These files replace
the earlier stroke approximation. The [reference image](langspan-reference.png)
is retained for context; the updated vector kit is the source for deployed assets.

The [palette](COLORS.txt) is warm white `#F4F3EF`, near-black `#151513`, slate
`#3F3E44` and coral `#D87859`. Dark-mode slate is `#A1A1AA`. The lockups have a
288 × 80 viewbox and the icons a 64 × 64 viewbox.

`langspan-avatar.png` is a 512 × 512 export of the supplied light icon, centered
on the warm background with padding for GitHub's crop. The same SVG artwork is
embedded in the product illustration and social preview. Both repositories
carry matching kit SVGs.
