## Parallel agentic development (25 November 2026)

Category: open-source coordination layer for existing coding agents. Primary headline: Run agents in parallel. Build as a team. Brand line: Your agents. On the same page. Lead with scale of independently operated sessions and semantic incompatibilities a clean merge can miss. The founder's five-local/five-cloud workflow inspires an illustration, not a verified LangSpan load test. Independent research motivates the problem; it is not product evidence. Native local refusal, stale retry and scoped commits, real two-machine briefing delivery, and scripted exact-tree integration checks remain distinct evidence categories. Local edit admission is refusal-oriented; queue/revalidation is a research hypothesis, not an implemented policy. Do not claim automatic semantic resolution, no data ever leaves an agent's machine, or measured rework reduction. Complement worktrees, do not replace them.

Use the maintainer's larger development runs as research context only until raw evidence is retained; do not advertise reported PR or refusal counts. Proposed wait/revalidation applies to active edit holds, not stale source; test by refusal kind. Benchmark should retain failures and compare 2/5/10 sessions, plain worktrees, awareness and full coordination. Concurrent rename-safety work belongs to engineering; website claims remain tied to published release or retained evidence.
